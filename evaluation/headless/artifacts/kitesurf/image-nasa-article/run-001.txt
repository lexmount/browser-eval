On Aug. 24, technicians at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida began installing
the four RS‑25 engines in the core stage of the agency’s Space Launch System
(SLS) rocket that will carry the [Artemis III ](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-json/ "text/javascript")crew into low Earth orbit in 2027.

Each RS‑25 engine has a unique serial number that records its detailed flight history.
The four engines assigned to Artemis III — E2054, E2057, E2048, and E2052 — previously
powered multiple space shuttle missions. Engine 2048 helped launch NASA astronaut
Randy Bresnik’s earlier mission aboard space shuttle [Atlantis](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-json/ "text/javascript") during STS‑129. It also flew on Space Shuttle Discovery during STS‑95 in 1998, the mission that returned 77-year‑old space pioneer [U.S. Sen. John Glenn](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-json/ "text/javascript") to orbit, making him the oldest person to fly in space at that time.
