| **English: ** "[The Blue Marble](/wiki/Commons:Copyrights\ "/wiki/Commons:Copyrights\")" is a famous photograph of the Earth taken on December 7, 1972, by the crew of the [Apollo 17](/wiki/Commons:Copyrights\ "/wiki/Commons:Copyrights\") spacecraft en route to the Moon at a distance of about 29,400 kilometres (18,300 mi).
