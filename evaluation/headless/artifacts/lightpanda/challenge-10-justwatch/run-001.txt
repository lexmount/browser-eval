# Discover TV shows and find where to watch online


From short miniseries to long-running TV dramas, nothing beats binge-watching a great
TV show. If you want to browse through the best TV shows available to stream, then
you can find everything you need with this guide. Select your streaming platforms
and browse through all of the series that are available to you.

## Where to watch TV series online?

By selecting your streaming services, you can use JustWatch to filter through your
whole library and find a great new show to binge right away. You can also check out
other streaming services to find out what’s available to buy, rent or watch for
free.

Follow all of the latest happenings in the Star Wars saga in shows like [The Mandalorian](https://images.justwatch.com "ssr") on [Disney+](/appassets/fonts/lato-regular.woff2), where you can also follow all of the TV shows in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Peek into the gothic world of [Wednesday](/appassets/fonts/lato-900.woff2) or watch the live action anime show [One Piece](/appassets/fonts/lato-900.woff2) on [Netflix](/appassets/favicon.ico). Puzzle over the mysteries of [Silo](https://www.googletagmanager.com/) on [Apple TV+](https://ib.adnxs.com) or watch the breakdown of the rich and wealthy in [Succession](https://www.justwatch.com/us/tv-shows) on [Max](https://www.justwatch.com/ar/series). [Amazon Prime Video](es-AR) has you covered if you follow the exploits of [Jack Ryan](https://www.justwatch.com/au/tv-shows) or want to visit Middle Earth with [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](https://www.justwatch.com/be/series).

JustWatch keeps you up to date with the latest in new seasons and episodes of your
favorite shows. From [Hulu](pt-BR), [Peacock](https://www.justwatch.com/ca/tv-shows), [Crunchyroll](https://www.justwatch.com/ch/Serien), [Paramount+](de-CH), and more, JustWatch has you covered.

## Watch series for free

If you’re looking to watch TV series for free online, there are a selection of
ad-supported streaming services that don’t charge a subscription fee. Platforms
like [Tubi](cs-CZ), [Pluto TV](de-DE), and [Freevee](https://www.justwatch.com/dk/tv-series) all host a variety of entertaining free series that can be streamed online. Most of these free streaming services don’t require you to sign up for an account. Instead, they show short adverts at different intervals, making the streaming experience much more like traditional TV.

You can also discover what series are available for free online by using the price
filter on JustWatch. Discover free series across all genres, or swap your search
to find free movies if that’s what you’re after.

## Find the best TV series to stream at JustWatch

Discovering the best TV series to watch online can be difficult. With JustWatch,
you can filter by IMDb rating and find popular TV series that match your search criteria.
So whether you’re looking to rewatch a classic series or looking for something
new, JustWatch can help you find the best TV series to stream online today.

JustWatch has several guides to help you find the best titles on streaming services.
Discover trending TV shows sorted by popularity or check out our guide to [new TV shows](hu-HU). Find out about [upcoming TV series](en-IE) and get a notification when they premiere. If you are also interested in watching movies online, you can find a complete guide to [movies and TV shows](ar-IQ) with JustWatch.
