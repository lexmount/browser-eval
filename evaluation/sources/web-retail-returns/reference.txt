Returns & Exchanges
Returning & Exchanging Online Orders
Return or exchange online orders by mail or at a store! Start your online return or exchange here. To locate your order number, simply review your order history. A $5.95 fee will be deducted from your refund for most mailed returns. If you prefer to return your item(s) at a store, you can find the nearest store location here.
Returning In-Store Purchases
In-store purchases must be returned to a store location. Locate a store near you to confirm business hours. Please note: Merchandise purchased at our stores cannot be returned via mail or to our warehouse.
Returning Furniture
Unlimited Furniture Delivery items in a saleable condition may be returned or exchanged within 30 days from the date of delivery. For item care and maintenance, visit our Furniture Guide or contact our furniture specialists to begin your return.

Returning an international order? Visit Returns & Exchanges Outside the US for more information.


Please note that all product-related issues, such as missing or damaged items/packages, must be reported within 30 days.
