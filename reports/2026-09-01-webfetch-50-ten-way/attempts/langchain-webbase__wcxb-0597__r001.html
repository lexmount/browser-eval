Our Story

While its saké is known for being elegant, thoughtful, and delicious, it is Hakutsuru's relentless drive for innovation that truly sets it apart. From studying water sources at the molecular level to building facilities capable of producing proprietary yeast strains—and even developing its own saké-specific rice, Hakutsuru Nishiki—the brand's deep commitment to research and development has propelled it to the forefront of a centuries-old craft.
