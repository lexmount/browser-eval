# [4o Update 2024-09-05 Broken](/t/4o-update-2024-09-05-broken/931828)

We’re on Teams and whatever version was released last night or today is broken. Document reviews, won’t do basic grammar checks, refers us to Grammatic of all things, errors in reviewing documents all over, is reverted to a five year old. I haven’t check all the rest we do, but will post more as we see it.

" **Contextual Conclusion**: My responses in this thread indicate that I struggled to maintain consistency in handling tasks that should have been well within my capabilities. The issues escalated when I introduced more complexity, such as automated grammar corrections, which led to incorrect outputs.
