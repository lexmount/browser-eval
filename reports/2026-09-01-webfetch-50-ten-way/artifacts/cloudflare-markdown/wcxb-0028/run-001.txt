## **Meta** information

Help search engines and AI models understand your pages

![meta information](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Meta-information helps search engines like Google correctly interpret, categorize, and display your content in search results.

 

The Seobility SEO Checker flags metadata issues, such as:

- Overly short, long, or missing meta titles and [descriptions](https://www.seobility.net/en/blog/meta-descriptions/)
- [meta tags](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Meta_Tags) that block indexing (noindex / nofollow)
- missing or incorrect [canonical links](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Canonical_Tag)
- inconsistent [language declarations](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Declaring_language_in_HTML)
- Unclear or problematic [domain names](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Domain_for_SEO) and [page URLs](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/SEO-friendly_URLs)

## Page **quality**

Create content that performs and gets cited

![page quality](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

High-quality, well-structured content is a core ranking factor for search engines like Google and is also more likely to be used in AI-generated answers.

 

The SEO Checker helps you identify page quality issues such as:

- Pages with too little text
- [Duplicate](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Duplicate_Content) or highly similar content
- Outdated HTML elements such as [framesets](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Frames_and_framesets)
- Poor [mobile optimization](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Mobile_Optimization)
- Images without descriptive [alt attributes](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/ALT_Attributes)
- Ads or overlays that discrupt content access

## Page and **link structure**

Improve crawlability for machines

![page and link structure](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Search engines and AI crawlers prefer websites that are easy to navigate and logically structured.

 

The SEO Checker detects issues that make it difficult for crawlers to analyze your website, including:

- Missing, empty or overly long or short [H1 headings](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/H1-H6_headings)
- Incorrect heading hierarchy
- [Internal links](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Internal_Linking) with dynamic parameters
- duplicate anchor texts of internal links
- Imbalanced internal and [external linking](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/External_Links)

## **Server** configuration

Ensure a fast, error-free technical setup

![server configuration](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

A clean technical foundation supports smooth crawling by search engine bots and AI crawlers. Fast-loading, error-free pages reduce friction for users and machines alike.

 

The SEO Checker scans for server errors and other technical issues like:

- Incorrect [redirect between www and non-www subdomains](https://www.seobility.net/en/redirectcheck/)
- [HTTP header](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/HTTP_headers) misconfigurations
- Slow server reponse times
- Excessive CSS or JavaScript requests
- Oversized HTML files

![server configuration](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## **External** factors

Strengthen trust and authority signals

![external factors](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

SEO and [AEO](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/answer-engine-optimization) are not only about on-page optimization. External signals, especially [backlinks](https://www.seobility.net/en/wiki/Backlinks) (also called “inbound links”), strongly influence Google rankings and citation likelihood in AI tools.

 

The SEO Checker will tell you if your page has a weak backlink profile.

## Frequently asked **questions**

What is the Seobility SEO Checker?

The Seobility SEO checker is a tool that scans any web page for technical errors, on-page SEO issues, and optimization gaps that affect search engine rankings and AI visibility.

How does the SEO Checker work?

The Seobility SEO checker crawls the entered URL like a search engine bot and **evaluates over 200 SEO-relevant factors** across metadata, content quality, structure, technical setup, and external signals.

 

It then calculates an SEO score for your page which reflects how well the page is currently optimized for Google and other search engines. It also displays a list of issues found on the page.

What is an SEO score?

The SEO score shows how well a page follows best practices:

 

- **80%+**: Strong SEO foundation
- **30 – 79%**: Optimization potential
- **Below 30%**: Critical issues requiring action

Can I download my SEO report?

Yes. Premium users can export branded PDF reports (short or full versions of the SEO Checker results), including their company logo.

 

Please visit our [pricing page](https://www.seobility.net/en/pricing/) for more information about our plans.

How do I improve my SEO score?

Follow the prioritized task list generated by the SEO Checker and focus first on high-impact issues on your website (displayed in red).

Can I check keyword rankings on Google too?

Yes. Use [Seobility’s Ranking Checker](https://www.seobility.net/en/rankingcheck/) to check your site’s keyword positions and compare your performance on Google against competitors.
