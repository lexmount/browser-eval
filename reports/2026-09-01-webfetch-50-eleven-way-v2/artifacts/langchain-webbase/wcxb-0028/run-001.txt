Free website checker

Get a quick overview of your website optimization






Analyze any web page with Seobility’s free SEO Checker to uncover technical errors and on-page SEO issues that limit organic rankings or reduce visibility in AI answer engines (LLM-based search tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity).
 
What you get:

Overall SEO score summarizing how well your page is currently optimized
Individual sub scores for meta-information, page quality, page & link structure, server configuration, and external signals
A prioritized task list with clear recommendations based on your SEO check results

 
The report highlights the highest-impact issues first so you can start with changes that deliver quick, measurable improvements.






















Meta information

Help search engines and AI models understand your pages






Meta-information helps search engines like Google correctly interpret, categorize, and display your content in search results.
 
The Seobility SEO Checker flags metadata issues, such as:

Overly short, long, or missing meta titles and descriptions
meta tags that block indexing (noindex / nofollow)
missing or incorrect canonical links
inconsistent language declarations
Unclear or problematic domain names and page URLs











Page quality

Create content that performs and gets cited






High-quality, well-structured content is a core ranking factor for search engines like Google and is also more likely to be used in AI-generated answers. 
 
The SEO Checker helps you identify page quality issues such as:

Pages with too little text
Duplicate or highly similar content
Outdated HTML elements such as framesets
Poor mobile optimization
Images without descriptive alt attributes
Ads or overlays that discrupt content access























Page and link structure

Improve crawlability for machines






Search engines and AI crawlers prefer websites that are easy to navigate and logically structured.
 
The SEO Checker detects issues that make it difficult for crawlers to analyze your website, including:

Missing, empty or overly long or short H1 headings
Incorrect heading hierarchy
Internal links with dynamic parameters
duplicate anchor texts of internal links
Imbalanced internal and external linking











Server configuration

Ensure a fast, error-free technical setup






A clean technical foundation supports smooth crawling by search engine bots and AI crawlers. Fast-loading, error-free pages reduce friction for users and machines alike.
 
The SEO Checker scans for server errors and other technical issues like:

Incorrect redirect between www and non-www subdomains
HTTP header misconfigurations
Slow server reponse times
Excessive CSS or JavaScript requests
Oversized HTML files























External factors

Strengthen trust and authority signals






SEO and AEO are not only about on-page optimization. External signals, especially backlinks (also called “inbound links”), strongly influence Google rankings and citation likelihood in AI tools.
 
The SEO Checker will tell you if your page has a weak backlink profile.

Frequently asked questions




What is the Seobility SEO Checker?



The Seobility SEO checker is a tool that scans any web page for technical errors, on-page SEO issues, and optimization gaps that affect search engine rankings and AI visibility.





How does the SEO Checker work?



The Seobility SEO checker crawls the entered URL like a search engine bot and evaluates over 200 SEO-relevant factors across metadata, content quality, structure, technical setup, and external signals. 
 
It then calculates an SEO score for your page which reflects how well the page is currently optimized for Google and other search engines. It also displays a list of issues found on the page.





What is an SEO score?



The SEO score shows how well a page follows best practices:
 

80%+: Strong SEO foundation
30 – 79%: Optimization potential
Below 30%: Critical issues requiring action






Can I download my SEO report?



Yes. Premium users can export branded PDF reports (short or full versions of the SEO Checker results), including their company logo.
 
Please visit our pricing page for more information about our plans.





How do I improve my SEO score?



Follow the prioritized task list generated by the SEO Checker and focus first on high-impact issues on your website (displayed in red).





Can I check keyword rankings on Google too?



Yes. Use Seobility’s Ranking Checker to check your site’s keyword positions and compare your performance on Google against competitors.
