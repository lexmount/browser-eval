# Professional Development

Florida Atlantic Executive Education Professional Development programs offer over
60 national and international certification programs—the most diversified selection
of professional development courses in South Florida.

For the fourth year in a row, the Financial Times ranks Florida Atlantic Executive
Education Professional Development Programs **[_No. 1 in Florida_](https://business.fau.edu/executive-education-financial-times-ranking.php)** in the 2026 Executive Education Open Enrollment ranking. Florida Atlantic also ranked
No. 4 in the United States.

Designed and taught by subject matter experts, our programs prepare working professionals
to master relevant knowledge, better their performance at work, diversify their expertise,
and further their careers. Our convenient and flexible courses and certification programs
are taught online, evenings, weekends or in boot camps to fit within busy lifestyles
with competing priorities.

Whether you are ready to become a Senior Human Resource Professional (SPHR)®, pursue the Project Management Professional (PMP)® certification, or aim to become a Certified Financial Planner (CFP)®, Florida Atlantic Executive Education Professional Development has the programs that
will help advance your career aspirations.
