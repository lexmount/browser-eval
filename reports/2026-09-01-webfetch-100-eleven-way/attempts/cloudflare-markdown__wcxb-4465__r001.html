## Hardware Tools for Reliable Results

A wide selection of tools is the basis of any good workshop or tool kit. Our range includes essentials needed in every home, garage or workshop, alongside more specialty items for handling advanced tasks.

- **Hand tools**: From multi-bit screwdrivers, pliers and hammers, to wrenches, handsaws, bolt cutters and more, find the [hand tools](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/hand-tools) you need to get any job done around your home.
- **Power tools & accessories**: For when you need some extra force, [power tools](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/power-tools) are there to help. Select between corded and cordless options, then  [shop power tool accessories ](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/power-tool-accessories)to get the most out of your equipment.
- **Welding and soldering tools**: When a project calls for high heat, [welding and soldering tools](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/welding-and-soldering-tools) offer a solution. Find welders and soldering guns, then shop protective gear for safe welding.
- **Fastening tools**: From staplers to rivets to glue guns and more, find [fastening tools](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/fastening-tools) to help you properly finish any job.
- **Air compressors and tools**: The versatility of an [air compressor](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/air-compressors-and-tools) makes it a must-have for any home handyman. For DIY car repairs and easy spray painting, air compressors make several difficult tasks easier.
- **Wet & dry vacuums**: Perfect for tackling a variety of cleanups, [wet dry vacuums](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/wet-dry-vacuums) dual-purpose vacuums do as their name suggests: clean up dry messes and remove wet spills. Use either at home, the garage or the job site.
- **Measuring and marking tools**: Work with accuracy using extendable locking [tape measures](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/measure-and-marking-tools) or advanced laser distancing measures to work with confidence.

Browse [tool storage](https://www.acehardware.com/departments/tools/tools-storage-and-organization) options to keep your work tools neatly organized and readily available on the go and in your workshop. Find toolboxes and bags to transport tools safely and securely from jobsite to jobsite, as well as larger storage cabinets for home tools in your garage.
