FAQs

Do DSLR lenses work on mirrorless cameras?

Most DSLR and mirrorless cameras have different lens mounts, even when produced by the same brand. Some brand-name and third-party adapters let you use DSLR lenses on mirrorless cameras. For example, the Canon EF-EOS R adapter accepts EF and EF-S lenses on R system camera bodies.

Do mirrorless cameras have interchangeable lenses?

In most cases, mirrorless cameras offer interchangeable lens capabilities. While many point-and-shoot cameras with integrated lenses are technically mirrorless, they're not usually considered true mirrorless cameras. Each of the flagship mirrorless cameras by Sony, Nikon, Canon, and other top brands offer the ability to swap lenses.

Do mirrorless cameras need special lenses?

Mirrorless cameras require lenses designed for their specific lens mount system. Because the lens mount of any camera contains electronic contact points for metering, autofocusing, image stabilization, and aperture adjustment, you need to select compatible lenses for your camera to enable full functionality.

How do you change lenses on a mirrorless camera?

Changing lenses on a mirrorless camera is simple once you get the hang of it. First, make sure to set your camera's power button to off. If your camera has a lens release button, press it down while twisting the currently attached lens to disconnect it from the camera. Next, line up the alignment points on the replacement lens and camera body, insert the end of the lens, and twist it into place. When it's seated correctly, you should feel it click into place.

## FAQs

### Do DSLR lenses work on mirrorless cameras?

Most DSLR and mirrorless cameras have different lens mounts, even when produced by the same brand. Some brand-name and third-party adapters let you use DSLR lenses on mirrorless cameras. For example, the Canon EF-EOS R adapter accepts EF and EF-S lenses on R system camera bodies.

### Do mirrorless cameras have interchangeable lenses?

In most cases, mirrorless cameras offer interchangeable lens capabilities. While many point-and-shoot cameras with integrated lenses are technically mirrorless, they’re not usually considered true mirrorless cameras. Each of the flagship mirrorless cameras by Sony, Nikon, Canon, and other top brands offer the ability to swap lenses.

### Do mirrorless cameras need special lenses?

Mirrorless cameras require lenses designed for their specific lens mount system. Because the lens mount of any camera contains electronic contact points for metering, autofocusing, image stabilization, and aperture adjustment, you need to select compatible lenses for your camera to enable full functionality.

### How do you change lenses on a mirrorless camera?

Changing lenses on a mirrorless camera is simple once you get the hang of it. First, make sure to set your camera’s power button to off. If your camera has a lens release button, press it down while twisting the currently attached lens to disconnect it from the camera. Next, line up the alignment points on the replacement lens and camera body, insert the end of the lens, and twist it into place. When it’s seated correctly, you should feel it click into place.
