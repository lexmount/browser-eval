These wireless headphones boast exceptional compatibility, seamlessly pairing with iOS and Android devices, as well as Bluetooth-enabled Windows laptops, tablets, and desktops, ensuring a hassle-free experience.
- 【Waterproof & Sweat-Resistant】: With IPX4-level protection, the earphones defend against perspiration after a workout and humidity on rainy days, ensuring their durability and performance remain uncompromised.
