# Beard Oil

Beard Oil is our award-winning, lightweight, and non-greasy moisturizing oil. Daily use helps prevent beard itch, soothe dry skin, and stop beard dandruff, leaving your beard healthy, soft, and smelling awesome. Formulated with natural abyssinian, jojoba, castor, and babassu oils, Beard Oil is non-comedogenic (won’t clog your pores) and safe for sensitive skin.
