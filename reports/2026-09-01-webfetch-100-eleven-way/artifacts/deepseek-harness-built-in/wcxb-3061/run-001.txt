## More about M5 chip and Performance

M5. The chip that zips.

The M5 chip in MacBook Air features a next-generation GPU with up to 10 cores, each with a powerful new Neural Accelerator. The M5 chip also includes a 10‑core CPU with faster multithreaded performance. It fuels all your demanding apps, including Microsoft 365 Copilot and Adobe Creative Cloud, making them run faster so you can be productive all day on a single charge.

   ![MacBook Air screen showing gaming to demonstrate M5 chip capabilities](/v/macbook-air/z/images/overview/performance/modal/modal_hero__dxdbuechqpea_large.jpg)

Neverness to Everness

_The M5_ chip makes _graphics smoother_ and gaming look better than ever. With third‑generation hardware-accelerated ray tracing, you get more realistic images for truly immersive gaming. And Dynamic Caching drives a huge performance boost for pro apps and games. So everything you do feels less like work and more like play.
