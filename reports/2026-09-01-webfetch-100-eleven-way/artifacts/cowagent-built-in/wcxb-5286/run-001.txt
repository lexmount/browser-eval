Eligibility criteria








We only rate 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organizations — these are US-based organizations commonly referred to as public charities that work all around the world. To receive an Encompass Rating, a charity must have three years of IRS Form 990 filings, which we receive directly from the IRS. For a complete Encompass Rating, charities must also submit data through the Charity Navigator Nonprofit Portal. We do not evaluate private foundations. Our site also includes basic information on charities that don&#8217;t meet our rating eligibility criteria. Our goal is to give you as much information as possible to guide your giving decisions.




What the ratings mean








Eligible charities receive a zero to four-star star rating — this is determined by the weighted sum of the organization’s metrics (0-100).
