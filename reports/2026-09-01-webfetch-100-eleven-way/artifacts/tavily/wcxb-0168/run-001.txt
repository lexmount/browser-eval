### Accelerate growth through sharper customer insights, AI-powered optimization, and actionable performance intelligence.

### Optimize the full funnel with advanced analytics

Acxiom helps brands improve acquisition, retention, growth and marketing efficiency through analytics solutions like Marketing Mix Modeling, Multi-Touch Attribution, look-alike modeling, churn indicators and detailed segmentation. We refine models and strategies based on evolving consumer behavior, market conditions and business goals, helping marketers make better decisions across every stage of the funnel.

### Turn first-party data into faster insights

With access to unified data, tools and global analytics talent, Acxiom helps brands unlock better, faster planning and insights from their first-party data. From customer journey and lifetime value analysis to marketing optimization and product innovation, we help marketers better understand their customers and deepen relationships over time.
