Beard Oil FAQs







              COMMON QUESTIONS
            
What makes Beardbrand Beard Oil so amazing?We use premium carrier oils that are non-comedogenic and have no impact on your body’s natural chemistry. Our Beard Oil is free of endocrine disruptors and does not inhibit the production of DHT (the testosterone byproduct that enables you to grow a beard).Learn more about the key ingredients in Beardbrand Beard Oil:Jojoba Oil: The chemical structure of Jojoba oil resembles sebum—that all-important oil your body naturally produces—making Jojoba oil an all-around banger of an ingredient for keeping dry, itchy skin at bay.Abyssinian Oil: Abyssinian oil is lightweight and absorbed quickly into hair and skin. Loaded with fatty acids and vitamins, Abyssinian oil helps improve hair texture and hydration. Oh yeah, all those antioxidants have been shown to reduce inflammation and slow down the effects of aging.Castor Oil: Castor oil forms a protective barrier on the skin, preventing too much moisture from escaping. It's also great for preventing split ends and softening hair while adding a healthy shine.Babassu Oil: Babassu oil is antimicrobial, anti-fungal, and anti-bacterial. Its moisturizing qualities help restore hair elasticity and health and aid in the prevention of water loss. Plus, this stuff is non-comedogenic (fancy talk for “doesn’t clog your pores”).Will Beard Oil make my beard grow thickerOur Beard Oil promotes beard growth and thickness by helping your beard and skin stay healthy, conditioned, and hydrated. This allows your beard to grow to the full potential of your genetic capabilities, giving it a thicker and fuller appearance. Beard growth is determined by genetics, and topical products like Beard Oil can’t change your beard-growing DNA.Is Beard Oil safe for all skin types?The active ingredients in Beardbrand Beard Oil all score between 0 and 2 on the comedogenic scale, meaning they are considered suitable for all skin types and have a low likelihood of clogging your pores.Note: the comedogenic scale is just one of many factors that help us predict how your skin will react to an ingredient. Ingredients derived from plants and fruits are often allergenic, but this is a potential side effect with all cosmetic products — not one that is solely exclusive to beard oil. Some people may experience allergic reactions to ingredients used in beard oils. Fragrance blends found in beard oils can also cause irritation for people with sensitive skin. Always spot-test a new beard oil to ensure your skin doesn’t react to it.How much Beard Oil do I get?Available in 1.7 fl oz / 50 ml or 4 fl oz / 118 ml
