Shipping and Returns

Shipping

We ship nationwide in the United States and Canada. Most in stock & credit-approved orders received by 2pm local time where order is being shipped from, will be processed the same day.

There may be a slight delay during sale periods due to high volumes. For more info see our shipping page.

Returns

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, please return the items for an exchange or credit.

All returns must be made within 30 days of the invoice date with a completed returns form - for full terms and conditions see our returns page.

Care Instructions

Machine wash cold with like colours. Do not bleach. Do not tumble dry. Do not dry clean. Do not iron if printed. Line dry in shade.

Made in Bangladesh.

Please note Army/Charcoal/Coal can be susceptible to fading/colouration variance. Also, particular sunblocks or deodorants mixed with high levels of sun exposure/body sweat can cause bleaching of the dyes in these colours - please consider.
